"Private by default" info banner: a white shield-with-eye glyph beside a bold title and one-line explainer on the purple-to-cyan brand gradient with a purple border. Default copy explains that a receive address is one-time and unlinkable to the user's @tag; `title` and `body` are overridable for other privacy explainers.

Place full-width on receive-via-link and deposit screens, above the address/QR block. Educational, not dismissible; for dismissible promos use `PromoBanner`.
