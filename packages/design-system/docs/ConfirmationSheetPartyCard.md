Counterparty card inside a confirmation sheet: avatar, gradient name over a muted handle, and the amount at the trailing edge. Pass a 40px `GradientInitialAvatar` in the `avatar` slot. `nameBadge` renders a small light-blue tag after the name (e.g. "Linked"); use `trailingColor="#56E79D"` with a `+` amount for incoming funds, and `trailingSubtitle` for a token caption under the amount.

Sits on a white-5% card fill with a hairline border, so it works on any sheet surface.
