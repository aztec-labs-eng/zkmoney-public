Top-of-sheet drag-handle capsule, 64x4, white at `opacity` (default 0.3; 0.35 for prominent sheets). A pure visual affordance: callers own the drag gesture.

Center it at the top of any bottom sheet or modal card. `SheetSurface` and `ConfirmationSheetShell` already include it, so add it manually only when building sheet chrome from scratch.
