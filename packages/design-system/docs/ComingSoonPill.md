Small brand-gradient capsule badge reading "Coming soon" (override with `label`). Marks payment methods and features that are visible but not yet available.

Its home is the trailing slot of a disabled `ListRow` with `floatingTrailing` so the badge floats top-right and the subtitle keeps full width. Pair with `disabled` on the row so the greyed text contrasts with the gradient pill.

`size="sm"` is the 10px tag that sits beside a 14px title, as "New" on a `HoldingRow`.
