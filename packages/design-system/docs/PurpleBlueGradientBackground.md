Full-bleed background with one large blurred purple-to-blue elliptical blob rotated across the upper-left of `#070707`. The single saturated blob gives a bolder, more directional wash than the aurora; used for welcome/onboarding surfaces.

Fills its positioned parent (`position: relative` + `overflow: hidden`). The blob is sized in container-width units against the 402pt design frame, so keep containers near phone aspect ratios. Content goes in `children`.
