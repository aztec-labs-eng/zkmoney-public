Gradient-masked text. `gradient="brand"` is the purple-to-cyan brand gradient (the "zk.money" label, defaults 12px/400); `gradient="title"` (default) is the white-to-gray title gradient used on section and card titles (defaults 20px/600). Override `size`, `weight`, and `family` ("display" or "body", both Sen) as needed.

Use brand sparingly: wordmark and brand accents only. For title-gradient text prefer the `TitleGradientText` alias.
