Translucent rounded surface (white @ 5%), the base container for row groups, transaction cards, and content panels over the dark canvas. Defaults to 12px radius and 12px padding; both are props.

Use it whenever content needs to lift off the background; pair with `TitleGradientText` for titled panels. Purpose-built cards (`ActivityListRow`, `TwoPartyAmountCard`) already include their own card chrome, so don't double-wrap them.
