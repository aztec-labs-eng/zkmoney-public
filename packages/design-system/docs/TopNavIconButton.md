40x40 glass-circle icon button for nav-bar slots: an `Icon` (16px, white) inside a `GlassCircleButton`. Pass `icon` (e.g. "search", "filter", "ellipsis") and an `ariaLabel`.

Use in the leading/trailing slots of `TopNavBar` and `ScreenNavBar`; cluster two or three with an 8px gap for multiple actions. For arbitrary content or a decorative (non-tappable) circle, drop down to `GlassCircleButton`.
