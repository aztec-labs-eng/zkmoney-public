A flow's steps on a rail inside a bordered box, under a caption and a "Step n of m" count. Done steps carry a green tick and a muted detail, the current step a turning gradient ring that stops once `failed` marks it failed, and the steps still to come a faint ring and gray label. The current step's detail is violet; `tone: "warning"` turns it gold for a delay or a closed prompt, `tone: "error"` pink for a wrong pick. The rail line grows with a detail that wraps.
