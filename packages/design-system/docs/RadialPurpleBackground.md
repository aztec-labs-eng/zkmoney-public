Full-bleed dark background with a purple-to-cyan radial glow bleeding past one edge of `#070707`. `anchor="top"` (default) puts the glow above the screen for hero/onboarding moments; `anchor="bottom"` grounds it under bottom-docked actions.

Fills its positioned parent (`position: relative` + `overflow: hidden`); put screen content in `children`. Louder than `AuroraBackground`, so reserve it for focused single-purpose screens like tag claiming.
