Full-width destructive action row: pink label with a trailing icon on a translucent pink surface. Use it at the bottom of detail sheets and settings screens for irreversible actions (delete wallet, cancel payment, remove contact). `icon` defaults to "trash"; pass "x-circle" for cancel-style actions.

Pair with `PrimaryGradientButton` (dark style) in confirm sheets where the destructive action is the highlighted choice.
