Label and value lines in a bordered box: what a step costs, how long it takes, what was charged. A fact's `hint` adds an info icon beside the label that shows a breakdown on hover, and keeps it open after a tap, click or Enter until a press or focus lands elsewhere; Escape closes it without closing the sheet. The breakdown opens below the label, or above it with `hint.above`.
