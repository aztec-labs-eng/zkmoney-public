One option of a single choice, drawn as a card with a radio, a label, an optional badge ("Recommended") and a description. A native radio sits inside, so cards that share a `name` form one keyboard-navigable group. The picked card gets the brand-gradient border and a purple tint. Children sit under the description as the option's help, such as a `SheetLink`: a press on its links, buttons, form fields or tooltips doesn't pick the card, and a press anywhere else in the card does.
