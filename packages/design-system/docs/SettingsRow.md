The single settings/profile row: optional glass icon circle, Sen-medium label, spacer, and an arbitrary trailing slot (a `RowChevron` on tappable rows, a muted value string, a `GradientToggle`, or a pill). `disabled` dims the label to 40% white and blocks the tap.

Group related rows inside a `GlassRowCard` (or `TitledGlassRowCard` with a section caption) rather than stacking them bare on the canvas.
