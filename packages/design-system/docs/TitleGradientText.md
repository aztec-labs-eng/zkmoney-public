White-to-gray gradient title text: `GradientText` locked to the title gradient. The standard treatment for section heads, card titles, contact-row handles, and hero amounts on dark surfaces.

Defaults to 20px/600 in Sen; pass `size`/`weight`/`family` to match the slot (e.g. 16px for card titles, `family="body"` for handles). Pairs with `Card` and the row components.
