Dark pill toast for transient feedback: a status icon (`kind`: `success` green check, `error` pink alert, `progress` purple spinner) beside the message, with an optional trailing `actionLabel` button or a dismiss ✕ (shown when `onDismiss` is set). The `progress` kind is the proving indicator ("Keeping it private…") while a transaction proves in the background.

Float it near the top of the screen over any background; keep messages to one short sentence.
