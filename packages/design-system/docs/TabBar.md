The bottom pill tab bar: a 63px glass capsule with a liquid-glass indicator that slides to the active tab. Pass `tabs` as `{name, label, icon}` (the app uses home / payments-arrows / history-clock / person for its four tabs) plus `activeTab` and `onTabChange`. Active tab text is white; inactive tabs are muted lavender.

Use `TabBarShell` when placing it at the bottom of a full screen; it adds the dark halo glow and edge padding.
