"✓✓" delivered/settled marker, default success green `#56E79D` at 14px. Sits beside chat-bubble timestamps ("11:15 ✓✓") and settled amounts to confirm a payment landed; override `color` to secondary grey for a quieter sent-but-unconfirmed treatment.

Always compose next to a timestamp or amount, never as a lone glyph. The in-flight counterpart is `Spinner`.
